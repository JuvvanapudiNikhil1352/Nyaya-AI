### Court Power to Re-open Loan Transactions (relevant_law)
In a suit for the recovery of a loan or enforcement of an agreement, a court may re-open the transaction if it believes the interest is excessive and the transaction was substantially unfair. If both conditions are met, the court may relieve you of liability for excessive interest and order the lender to repay any excess amount paid. The court determines if interest is excessive by looking at the risk, security, charges, and financial circumstances at the time of the loan.
